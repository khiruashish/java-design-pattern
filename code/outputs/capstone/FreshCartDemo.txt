=== FreshCart: a grocery platform built from the handbook's patterns ===

1) Catalog [Builder] [Flyweight] [Proxy]
   6 lookups -> 2 database reads (caching proxy)
   4 products share 3 category objects (flyweight)
   builder validation: BAD-1: name, category and a positive price are required

2) Cart [Command]
   after removing milk: 2 x Alphonso mangoes 1 kg; 1 x Basmati rice 5 kg
   undid: remove 3 x Toned milk 1 L
   cart: 2 x Alphonso mangoes 1 kg; 1 x Basmati rice 5 kg; 3 x Toned milk 1 L | subtotal ₹1,615.00

3) Pricing [Specification] [Strategy]
   FRUIT10 -> subtotal ₹1,615.00 - discount ₹79.80 [FRUIT10] + GST ₹40.85 + delivery ₹0.00 = ₹1,576.05
   FLAT75  -> subtotal ₹1,615.00 - discount ₹75.00 [FLAT75] + GST ₹40.85 + delivery ₹0.00 = ₹1,580.85

4) Checkout [Facade] [Chain of Responsibility] [Saga] [Factory Method] [Adapter] [Circuit Breaker] [Outbox]

      [Saga] book delivery slot ✔
      [Saga] charge ₹1,576.05 via UPI ✔
      [Saga] save order + outbox event ✔

   orders table: 1, outbox rows: 1
