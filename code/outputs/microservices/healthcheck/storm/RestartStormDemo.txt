=== Challenge: The Database Belongs in Readiness, Not Liveness ===
DB in liveness : restarts=40, pods able to serve at t=120s (DB back since t=110s): 0/20
DB in readiness: restarts=0, pods able to serve at t=120s (DB back since t=110s): 20/20
Restarting can't fix a dependency; it only adds cold starts when the dependency returns.
